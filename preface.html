About the Book

Why This Story Matters

The Other Side of the Story: LGBTQIA+ Edition

Some stories are difficult to tell because they ask us to look beyond what we have always believed we understood.

Relationships are complicated. Faith can raise difficult questions. Family responsibilities create circumstances that require patience and understanding. Identity can shape how people experience the world, but no single part of a person’s identity tells the entire story of who they are.

This edition was developed to bring those realities into the same conversation.

The original message of The Other Side of the Story centered on the importance of understanding different perspectives within relationships. This edition carries that message into experiences that include LGBTQIA+ identity, committed same-sex relationships, family life, and Christian faith.

The relationship at the center of these pages belongs to two men who are learning to love one another through the ordinary and extraordinary parts of life. Their experiences are not intended to represent every LGBTQIA+ relationship. They are one story, offered as a way to explore themes that reach far beyond any single relationship.

The chapters examine the assumptions people make, the things they leave unsaid, the expectations that become disappointments, and the hurt that can remain hidden long after an argument has ended. They also explore the importance of accountability, boundaries, trust, affection, and the willingness to grow together.

Alongside those relationship themes is a conversation about faith. For some readers, the question of being LGBTQIA+ and Christian may be deeply personal. For others, it may be unfamiliar or difficult to understand. Christian communities do not all interpret Scripture concerning same-sex relationships alike. This book acknowledges those differences while encouraging readers to approach one another with dignity and sincerity.

The purpose is not to force agreement. The purpose is to encourage understanding.

A person should not have to explain every part of his existence before someone recognizes his humanity. A couple should not be reduced to the challenges they experience. A family should not be judged through assumptions made by someone who has never understood its circumstances.

As you read, consider the conversations that have remained unfinished in your own life. Think about the people whose experiences may differ from yours. Consider whether you have listened carefully enough to understand what someone was actually trying to communicate.

You may not agree with every perspective presented in these pages. That is not a requirement for reading with an open mind.

What matters is the willingness to recognize that another person may be carrying a story you have never fully heard.

There is more to every relationship, more to every person, and always more to the story.
