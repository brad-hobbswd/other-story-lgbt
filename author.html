About the Author

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs is an educator and writer whose work is shaped by an interest in human relationships, communication, personal growth, and the experiences that influence how people understand one another.

Through his writing, Bradley explores the space between assumptions and understanding. His work invites readers to look beyond first impressions, listen carefully to perspectives different from their own, and recognize the dignity of the people behind every story.

The Other Side of the Story: LGBTQIA+ Edition brings those interests into a conversation about relationships, family, identity, and Christian faith. The book encourages thoughtful reflection while making room for honest questions, differing perspectives, and the real lives represented within its pages.

Bradley hopes readers will approach each story with curiosity, compassion, and a willingness to understand more than what may first appear.
