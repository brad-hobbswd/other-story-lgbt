The heart behind the book

Every story deserves to be heard.

Understanding begins when we are willing to listen beyond assumptions and see the people behind the stories.

The Other Side of the Story: LGBTQIA+ Edition explores love, family, faith, and the realities of building a life together. Through the experiences of two men navigating relationship, responsibility, and Christian belief, this book invites readers to consider another perspective with honesty, compassion, and respect.

You do not have to share someone’s experience to treat their story with dignity.
