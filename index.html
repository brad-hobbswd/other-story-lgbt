The Other Side of the Story

LGBTQIA+ Edition

Love, Faith, Family, and the Questions We Are Afraid to Ask

Two people. One relationship. Different experiences.

Every relationship holds more than one perspective.

We remember what happened through our own eyes. We explain what hurt us, defend what we meant, and sometimes overlook what the other person was experiencing. Yet every relationship contains experiences, expectations, and emotions that deserve to be heard.

This edition brings those familiar relationship questions into a story that includes LGBTQIA+ identity, committed love, family life, and Christian faith. Through the experiences of two men, the book considers communication, assumptions, accountability, boundaries, trust, and the work of growing together.

The story is one relationship, not a definition of every LGBTQIA+ experience. Its invitation is broader: listen carefully, look beyond assumptions, and make room for the parts of another person's story you may not yet understand.
