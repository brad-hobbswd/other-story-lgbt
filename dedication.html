Dedication This book is dedicated to every person who has ever wondered whether there was room for all of who they are. To those who have loved deeply, questioned honestly, prayed privately, and searched for a place where their hearts could be understood. To the men and women who have experienced the beauty of love while carrying questions about faith, identity, family, and belonging. To those who have been misunderstood, judged by assumptions, or defined by a single part of their story. May you never forget that your life is more than someone else’s interpretation of who you are. To those who have chosen love while continuing to seek God, who have held onto their faith through difficult questions, and who believe that compassion and understanding still have a place in every conversation. And to everyone who has ever needed someone to listen before making a judgment. Your story matters. Your heart matters. There is always more to your story.
