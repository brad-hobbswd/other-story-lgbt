Acknowledgements A book about relationships, understanding, faith, and love is never simply about the words written on its pages. It reflects the experiences, questions, conversations, and moments of reflection that remind us how much there is to learn about one another. I am grateful for every person whose life, experiences, and willingness to speak honestly have helped shape the message behind this book. Every conversation that challenges an assumption, every act of kindness that restores someone’s confidence, and every moment when someone chooses understanding over judgment reinforces the importance of telling stories with honesty and compassion. I am especially grateful for the people who continue to believe that love and faith deserve thoughtful conversations. The journey of understanding is not always comfortable, and asking difficult questions does not always produce immediate answers. Still, those conversations matter. To the LGBTQIA+ community, thank you for continuing to share your stories, your experiences, your hopes, and your desire to be understood beyond the labels others may place upon you. To families, partners, friends, and faith communities willing to listen with open hearts, thank you for recognizing that people need more than acceptance in words. They need respect, genuine connection, and the freedom to live without constantly defending their humanity. To my readers, thank you for opening these pages and allowing this story to become part of your own reflection. Whether you recognize yourself in these chapters or are discovering experiences different from your own, I hope you leave with a deeper appreciation for the importance of listening. Above all, I give thanks to Jesus Christ, whose example of love, compassion, humility, and grace continues to shape my understanding of how we should treat one another. Thank you for being part of this journey. Bradley Hobbs
