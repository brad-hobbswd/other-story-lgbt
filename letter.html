The Other Side of the Story · LGBTQIA+ Edition

Letter From the Author

Dear Reader,

There are some books we write because we have something to say. Others come from questions that deserve more attention than they are often given. This book was written with both purposes in mind.

The Other Side of the Story: LGBTQIA+ Edition was created to explore the experiences that can exist behind relationships, identities, family circumstances, and questions of faith. The intention was never to suggest that every relationship is perfect, that every disagreement has an easy answer, or that everyone must arrive at the same understanding of life. The intention was to create room for a more honest conversation.

At the heart of this book is a relationship between two men who love one another and are learning how to build a life together. They experience affection, laughter, frustration, responsibility, and the ordinary challenges that come with sharing life with another person. They are not defined by their sexual orientation alone. They are individuals with hopes, responsibilities, families, convictions, and a genuine desire to love one another well.

Their story also includes faith.

One of the questions surrounding this edition is one that many people have asked, sometimes privately and sometimes openly: Can someone be gay, genuinely love Jesus Christ, follow Him, and still have a place in Heaven?

I recognize that Christians hold differing interpretations of Scripture concerning same-sex relationships. Those differences are real, and they deserve honest discussion. However, I also believe that difficult theological conversations should never become permission to disregard another person’s dignity, humanity, or personal journey with God.

A person’s life cannot be reduced to a single question. Neither can a relationship be fully understood through someone else’s assumptions.

I hope this book encourages readers to consider the difference between what they believe they know about another person and what they have actually taken the time to understand. I hope couples recognize the importance of communication, accountability, boundaries, affection, and the continued effort required to maintain a healthy relationship. I hope families discover that listening can open doors that judgment often closes.

Most importantly, I hope LGBTQIA+ readers recognize that their stories deserve to be told with honesty and care. You are more than the questions people ask about you. You are more than the assumptions others make. Your relationships, your experiences, your faith, and your humanity deserve to be approached with thoughtfulness and respect.

Thank you for allowing me to share these pages with you. My hope is that you will finish this book not simply with another perspective, but with a greater willingness to understand the person whose story may be different from your own.

There is always more to the story.

With gratitude,

Bradley Hobbs
