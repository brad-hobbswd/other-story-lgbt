Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected when you visit The Other Side of the Story: LGBTQIA+ Edition website, published by Bradley Hobbs.

Website hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, device information, and access logs to deliver, secure, and maintain the hosting service. GitHub's handling of information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general website traffic and how visitors use the pages. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate geographic region, device and browser information, referral sources, interactions, and identifiers stored through cookies or similar technologies. The information helps evaluate site performance and improve the visitor experience.

Google may process information in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Cookies and analytics choices

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. You may also use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking cookies may affect some analytics measurements.

Information submitted by visitors

At present, the website does not offer visitor accounts or online purchases. Should contact forms, mailing lists, or other submission features be added, this policy will be updated to explain what information is requested and how that information is used.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit personal information about a child through the website.

Changes and contact

This policy may be revised when website features or practices change. The effective date above will be updated when revisions are made. For privacy questions, use the contact method published by the author or publisher where available.
